# Arctechtire Showroom — Customer FAQ

## Visiting hours
The showroom is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.
Visits are by appointment so that a product specialist can give you their full attention. Each visit lasts about one hour.

## Location and parking
We are located on the main commercial road with free customer parking behind the building. Please carry a photo ID for entry.

## Products
We offer car, SUV, two-wheeler and commercial vehicle tyres from leading brands. Our specialists can recommend tyres based on your vehicle model, driving style, and budget.
Alloy wheels and wheel alignment and balancing services are also available.

## Pricing and offers
Tyre prices depend on brand, size and type. Final prices are confirmed at the showroom after checking your vehicle. Seasonal discounts and exchange offers may be available; ask the specialist during your visit.

## Warranty and returns
All tyres carry the manufacturer's warranty against manufacturing defects. Keep your invoice. Fitting defects are covered for 30 days from purchase.

## What to bring
Bring your vehicle registration (RC) so we can verify the correct tyre size, and your vehicle if you want a fitting or alignment check on the same visit.

## Rescheduling and cancellation
You can cancel or reschedule a visit up to two hours before the slot, using your booking ID and the phone number you registered with.
